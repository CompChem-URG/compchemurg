# CompChemURG

**CompChemURG** (Computational Chemistry Underrepresented Groups) is a community for underrepresented groups in computational chemistry. We aim to connect, support, and amplify members of underrepresented groups working in and around computational chemistry, through community-building, mentorship, and events.
